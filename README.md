# 📉 Customer Churn Analysis in R

This project uses logistic regression to predict customer churn based on service usage patterns and contract details. The goal is to uncover actionable insights that can help a telecommunications company reduce churn and improve customer retention.

## 🔍 Objective

- Predict whether a customer will churn
- Identify key drivers of churn
- Provide business recommendations based on data

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Language:** R
- **Methods:** Logistic Regression
- **Libraries:** `ggplot2`, `dplyr`, `caret`, `pROC`
- **Data:** 7,000+ anonymized customer records (tenure, contract, monthly charges)

## 📊 Key Findings

- Customers with longer tenure are significantly less likely to churn
- One- and two-year contracts reduce churn risk by 65–86%
- Higher monthly charges increase churn likelihood by ~2.9% per dollar

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Incentivize long-term contracts to improve retention
- Focus onboarding and engagement on early-tenure customers
- Offer pricing bundles or loyalty perks to reduce churn sensitivity
